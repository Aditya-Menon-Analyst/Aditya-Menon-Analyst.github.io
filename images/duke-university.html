About this destination

Duke University, located in Durham, North Carolina, is a renowned private research university known for its outstanding academic programs, distinguished faculty, and vibrant campus life. Established in 1838, the university was originally known as Trinity College before adopting its current name in 1924, in honor of the Duke family, whose generous endowment transformed it into a nationally recognized institution. The university is divided into ten schools and colleges, offering a wide range of undergraduate, graduate, and professional programs. Its most notable schools include the Pratt School of Engineering, the Fuqua School of Business, and the School of Law.

Duke is ranked No. 8 in the nation’s university ranking, and I think its campus should be equally high-ranking with many mature trees, immaculate lawns, and beautiful Gothic architecture. If you are on campus during lunch or dinner time, try one of the cafes and restaurants. You won’t be disappointed. For us, visiting Duke University was a highlight of our time in Durham.

Beyond academics and research, Duke University emphasizes the importance of community engagement and global outreach. It hosts a diverse student body from all 50 states and over 100 countries. The university encourages its students to engage in civic activities and offers various international programs and partnerships. This commitment to a global perspective reflects Duke's mission to cultivate leaders who are prepared to make a positive impact in an increasingly interconnected world.

Durham is not only home to Duke University but also features many exciting activities and attractions. Visitors can explore the beautiful Duke Gardens, a 55-acre botanical garden located right on the university campus. The gardens showcase a variety of plants, flowers, and serene landscapes, making it a perfect spot for relaxation or a leisurely walk. Additionally, the Nasher Museum of Art offers a rich collection of contemporary and historic art, including works from Africa, Europe, and the Americas. This museum frequently hosts special exhibitions and events, further enriching the cultural scene in Durham.

Another notable landmark is the Duke Chapel, an iconic structure on campus. This stunning Gothic-style chapel stands as a symbol of the university and is open to the public. People are welcome to visit and enjoy the breathtaking stained glass windows and the towering spire, which can be seen from miles away. The chapel often hosts concerts and community events, making it a vibrant part of campus life.

Visitors to Durham will find a variety of accommodations to suit different preferences and budgets. The Washington Duke Inn & Golf Club offers luxury hotel accommodations with beautiful grounds and close proximity to the university. It's an excellent choice for guests looking for high-end comfort and amenities. For a more budget-friendly experience, the Crowne Plaza Durham is conveniently located and provides comfortable rooms along with easy access to highways and local attractions.

Dining is another highlight of Durham. The city has a thriving food scene known for its Southern cuisine, farm-to-table restaurants, and barbecue joints. The historic Brightleaf Square district features several eateries and shops housed in refurbished tobacco factories. Here, guests can enjoy meals at popular spots like the Parisian-inspired Blu Seafood and the acclaimed M Restaurant.

Durham also acts as a gateway to nearby attractions. Just a short drive away is the enchanting Eno River State Park, where visitors can hike, kayak, or picnic amidst the beautiful forest and riverside landscapes. For those interested in history, the nearby Historic Hillandale Neighborhood showcases well-preserved homes reflecting the city’s architecture from the early 20th century.

Overall, Duke University and its surrounding areas in Durham provide a diverse array of activities, cultural experiences, and comfortable accommodations, making it a destination rich in tradition and modernity. Whether it is enjoying the university's programs or exploring the local landmarks, visitors will find plenty to see and do in this vibrant North Carolina town.
